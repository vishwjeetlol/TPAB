About the Author

Vishwjeet Chavan

Vishwjeet Chavan is an author, cultural critic, and researcher writing at the crossroads of contemporary music, African American literature, and socio-political theory. His work focuses on the deconstruction of modern hip-hop concept albums as high-art literary and cinematic texts.

To Pimp a Butterfly: A Critical and Cultural Reading represents the culmination of extensive archival research, musicological transcription, and cultural synthesis into Kendrick Lamar’s landmark 2015 masterpiece.
